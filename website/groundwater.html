Executive Summary

This project evaluates groundwater quality using heavy metal pollution indices, multivariate statistical techniques and spatial analysis. Python was used to automate statistical analysis while GIS was used to visualize the spatial distribution of contaminants and identify areas of elevated groundwater pollution.

Project Objectives

  • Determine the concentration of selected heavy metals in groundwater.
  • Evaluate groundwater quality using HPI, HEI and Metal Index.
  • Identify relationships among heavy metals using correlation analysis.
  • Apply Principal Component Analysis and Cluster Analysis.
  • Produce spatial distribution maps using GIS.

Study Area

Groundwater samples were collected from selected boreholes within the study area in Obuasi, Ghana. Geographic coordinates were recorded for each sampling location to enable spatial interpolation and mapping.

Results

  • Heavy metal concentrations were successfully quantified.
  • Pollution indices identified areas of concern.
  • Correlation analysis revealed relationships among several metals.
  • PCA identified the dominant controlling factors.
  • Cluster analysis grouped similar groundwater samples.
  • GIS maps highlighted the spatial distribution of contamination.

Lessons Learned

  • Python greatly reduced repetitive statistical calculations.
  • GIS improved visualization of groundwater contamination.
  • Multivariate statistics provided deeper interpretation than descriptive statistics alone.
  • Combining Python and GIS creates a powerful groundwater assessment workflow.
