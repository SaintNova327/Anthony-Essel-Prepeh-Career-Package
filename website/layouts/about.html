Career Journey

My journey into Geological Engineering began with a deep curiosity about the Earth, its resources and the role geology plays in solving engineering and environmental challenges. Throughout my studies at Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), I have developed practical knowledge through field mapping, laboratory work, technical reporting and independent research.

As my interests evolved, I became increasingly drawn to digital technologies used in modern geoscience. This led me to explore Geographic Information Systems (GIS), remote sensing, geological modelling, hydrogeology, data analysis and artificial intelligence as tools for improving mineral exploration, groundwater investigations and modern mining workflows.

Beyond my academic work, I continuously invest in professional development through industry certifications, technical software training, programming and personal engineering projects. My goal is to become a geological engineer who combines strong scientific knowledge with modern digital technologies to solve real-world challenges in the mining and environmental sectors.
