# Orebody Modelling using Leapfrog Geo
### Project Workflow

---

## Step 1 — Geological Core Logging

**Images**
- 01_core_logging.jpg

**Description**

Drill core trays were systematically arranged and logged to identify lithology, geological structures, alteration, mineralization, and other geological characteristics required for subsequent geological interpretation.

---

## Step 2 — Standardized Drill Core Photography

**Images**
- 02_camera_setup.jpg
- 03_core_photography.jpg

**Description**

A Canon EOS 5D camera mounted on a fixed horizontal frame was used to capture standardized, high-resolution photographs of the drill cores for geological documentation and interpretation.

---

## Step 3 — Digital Core Management

**Images**
- 04_core_management.jpg

**Description**

The captured drill core photographs were organized into a structured digital database, enabling efficient cataloguing, management, retrieval, and interpretation of geological images in a workflow similar to industry image-management systems such as IMAGO.

---

## Step 4 — Digital Terrain Model Preparation

**Images**
- 05_Digital_elevation_model.jpg
- 06_Aerial_Photograph.jpg

**Description**

A Digital Elevation Model (DEM) was generated in QGIS using interpolation techniques. Aerial imagery was then integrated with the DEM to provide topographic context for geological modelling.

---

## Step 5 — Topography Construction in Leapfrog Geo

**Images**
- 07_creating_topography.jpg

**Description**

The processed DEM was imported into Leapfrog Geo to create the project topographic surface, providing the three-dimensional framework for geological modelling.

---

## Step 6 — Drillhole Database Import

**Images**
- 08_leapfrog_drillhole_import.jpg

**Description**

Validated drillhole collar, survey, lithology, and assay data were imported into Leapfrog Geo and checked for consistency before geological interpretation.

---

## Step 7 — Geological Interpretation and Modelling

**Images**
- 09_modelling.jpg

**Description**

Geological domains, lithological contacts, and stratigraphic relationships were interpreted and modelled to construct a realistic three-dimensional geological representation.

---

## Step 8 — Final Geological Model

**Images**
- 10_final_model.jpg

**Description**

The completed three-dimensional geological model provided a clear visualization of the subsurface geology and demonstrated the complete workflow from drill core logging through geological interpretation to 3D modelling.

---
